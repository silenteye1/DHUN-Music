## Features ✨️    
- Play music from YouTube Music or YouTube for free, without ads and in the background
- Multiple Now Playing styles: Classic, Material 3 Expressive and Apple Music
- Ten-band equalizer with presets and AutoEq headphone profiles
- On-device listening analytics: charts, period history, and listening clock
- Word-by-word synchronized lyrics and share lyrics as an image
- Home screen widgets for quick access and playback control
- High-quality audio streaming options
- Browse Home, Charts, Moods, and Genres with high speed
- Search everything on YouTube
- Spotify Canvas supported
- Play video option with subtitle support
- AI song suggestions
- Import playlists easily
- Caching and offline playback support
- Crossfade and custom themes (Light, Dark, Color, etc.)
- Supports SponsorBlock and Return YouTube Dislike
- Sleep Timer
- Android Auto integration and Discord Rich Presence support
- And many more!    
  
## Tech Stack & Architecture
* **Language:** Kotlin
* **UI Framework:** Jetpack Compose & Compose Multiplatform
* **Architecture:** Modern MVVM and clean architecture with reactive streams.

## Privacy & Control
**DHUN-Music** is independently managed to give you full control over your app experience. All third-party update prompts, external funding links, and original developer dependencies have been completely stripped out.
